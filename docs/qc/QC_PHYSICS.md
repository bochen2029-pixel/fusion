## 2 · Cross-pollination log

**Confirmed (peer → me):**

- **QC-CONTROL B1 (no passive structure).** CONFIRMED — independently and simultaneously derived as PHY-03 (posted #634 before I read their #632). Added evidence they did not have: the machine-by-machine growth-rate ordering tracks **wall thickness, not κ** (SPARC κ≈1.97 yet γ ≤ 100 s⁻¹ because of a deliberately thick vessel; DIII-D thin vessel 100–400 s⁻¹; ITER 5–20 s⁻¹) — only explicable if γ is set by the passive structure's L/R. Added two requirements to their fix: build the mutual-inductance matrix **general** (it carries quench eddy/halo forces later), and **report γ from the eigenproblem** rather than configuring it, because it moves with l_i and κ through a ramp and their null's gains must schedule on it.
- **QC-CONTROL B3 (innovation event rate).** CONFIRMED their 40 ch × 10 kHz assumption from the diagnostics side — magnetics genuinely are sampled at 10–50 kHz on real machines (integrated dB/dt). Two physics refinements contributed: (a) magnetic innovations are **strongly correlated across the array** (one plasma motion moves all 40), so per-channel z-tests fire in bursts of ~40 — which makes their per-cluster Mahalanobis fix the only *statistically correct* form, not merely the cheaper one; (b) non-magnetic channels are far slower (neutron ~1 kHz, interferometer line-integrated, Dα 10–100 kHz), so a single `sigma_token` for all clusters is wrong — thresholds must be per-cluster.
- **QC-CONTROL B5 (clairvoyance trap / two fork semantics).** CONFIRMED, and made cheaper: most §2.3 disturbances are **not exogenous** — a sawtooth crash is state-triggered (q₀ crossing 1, Porcelli-type criterion) and an ELM is triggered by the pedestal crossing a peeling-ballooning boundary. Model those as state-triggered (which is also the honest physics) and the teacher cannot know their timing any better than the policy can; Philox then drives only the genuinely exogenous faults (NBI trip, coil failure, impurity influx, sensor faults), which is a much smaller set for their decorrelated-key rule to cover. Their ghost-fork-shares-stream / planner-rollout-re-keys split stands and I back it.
- **QC-VOICE-RT (no GPU arbitration policy).** CONFIRMED and escalated with a plant-side number: a tier-1 GS solve is 2–7 ms of GPU work that must land inside a 5–10 ms window; under WDDM time-slicing against a ~17 ms LLM decode step it can be preempted past its window, after which the 10 kHz linearised vertical model runs on a **stale linearisation**. At the corrected γ⁻¹ = 10–50 ms a 20–30 ms stale linearisation is a control-relevant modelling error. **GPU contention therefore corrupts the plant, not merely the voice latency** — which raises the severity of their finding. Added requirement: a missed GS window must emit a `[sys]` event, because the memcmp oracle is deterministic in *sim* time and structurally blind to this class of *wall*-time failure.
- **QC-REPO-PROCESS (q95 comparator direction).** CONFIRMED physically: q95 is a **lower** limit approached from above (2.6 → 2.35 → floor 2.2 is the correct ordering; the missing thing is the comparator), while Greenwald and β_N are upper limits approached from below. Their `dir = "above"|"below"` key is the right fix. Added: there is **no lower density bound** in `floors.toml`, and low density is a genuine hazard (runaway-electron-prone, L-mode back-transition) — "the mind cannot cross" should have a floor in that direction too.
- **QC-MEMBRANE (no event token for disruption/VDE/quench).** CONFIRMED and paired with my PHY-06: the spec has **neither the event nor the physics** of the plasma dying. Fixing either alone yields a token that can never fire, or a death the mind cannot name. Their proposed `[terminal]` group and my disruption-model fix should land together.
- **QC-MEMBRANE / QC-REPO-PROCESS (Greenwald 0.95 vs 1.0).** Both asked PHYSICS to rule. **Ruling issued on the bus and recorded as PHY-23: the table (0.95) is correct and §4.2's prose is stale** — and the table's *comment* is also wrong physics (n_GW is an empirical disruptive boundary, not a "physical limit"; H-mode with pellets runs above it).

**Corrected by me, mid-audit (self-refutation on the record):** my first PHY-02 (bus #630) proposed pinning a SPARC-class machine as the fix that makes ms-scale VDE and Q>1 coexist. **That was wrong** and I retracted it in #633 after checking the literature: SPARC's vertical growth rate is γ ≲ 100 s⁻¹ *because* it has a deliberately thick conducting wall for disruption forces. The general principle — burning-plasma machines need thick walls, and the wall's L/R time *is* the growth time — means **no machine is both Q>1-capable and ms-VDE**. ## 3 · Verdict

**Is §2 buildable as specced by a cold session? Tier-0/M0: yes, but the gate is not passable as written. Tier-1/M1: no — three load-bearing physics elements are simply absent.**

M0 is close. The tier-0 formula list is well-chosen and correctly named (Bosch-Hale, alpha heating, Bremsstrahlung, Trubnikov synchrotron, IPB98(y,2), Lawson/Q is a coherent minimal burn model, and it is the right one). But the milestone as written **cannot pass its own gate for three independent reasons that are all spec defects, not code defects**: no machine is pinned so "Q > 1" may be physically unreachable (PHY-01); nothing is stochastic so the Wilson CI is degenerate (PHY-05); and the gate has no duration or pass fraction (PHY-16). Add the missing dynamics that make the sustain-Q game a game at all — particle balance, helium ash, dilution (PHY-07) — and M0 becomes both passable and worth passing. All four fixes are a few hours of spec work and cost the cold session nothing extra to implement; **not fixing them costs a milestone**.

M1 is not buildable as written. The vertical instability — the project's declared antagonist and the justification for the entire kHz architecture — has **no model of the conductor that makes it exist** (PHY-03); the current profile that free-boundary GS requires as an *input* is never specified (PHY-14); and disruption, the terminal state the constitution promises is "fully simulated", has no physics at all (PHY-06). A session that starts M1 from this document will stall at the first `TODO: where does p′(ψ) come from?` or, worse, hard-code a growth rate and quietly forfeit the "hard-to-fake by construction" property that is the whole inheritance from the booster.

**Top 3 risks.**
1. **The machine is never pinned, so every number in the spec is floating** — and the two numbers that *are* stated (γ⁻¹ ~ 1–5 ms, and a Q>1 gate) belong to two different machines that cannot be the same machine. This propagates into floors, ramp rates, scenario durations, MC wall-clock cost, and the demo script. Fix PHY-01 + PHY-02 first; roughly a third of the other findings dissolve.
2. **The multi-rate law (D-003) was applied to the plant but not to everything downstream of it.** The ghost budget (PHY-04), F-GHOST's memcmp (PHY-04), F-KEEPUP-F's per-tick definition (PHY-11), MPPI rollout cost, and the §7 plant row were all written against a single-rate mental model. D-003 is correct; it just needs to be pushed through the rest of the document, and one of its consequences (frozen-equilibrium ghosts) changes a **falsifier**, which is exactly the kind of change this project's own doctrine says must be made deliberately and in advance.
3. **The scenario set is a demo script, not a curriculum.** No VDE, no ramp-down, no H→L back-transition (PHY-12); `breakdown` has no model (PHY-13); `impurity puff → radiative collapse` is unreachable with Brems-only radiation (PHY-08). M1's gate is "the oracle lands flat-top through the disturbance set" — if the disturbance set omits the one disturbance the controller exists to reject, M1 can pass green while proving nothing, and M2's F-NULL-C will then compare a net and a PID on a problem where neither is stressed.

**Answer to QC-REPO-PROCESS's question ("is your section's gate testable as written by a session
that has only this repo?"):** **No, for both M0 and M1.** Their observation is correct and load-
bearing — §8 defines no falsifier for M0 or M1, so DEFINITION_OF_DONE clause 1 ("the gate
falsifier is green") is *undefined for exactly the two milestones a cold session runs first*. M0's
stated gate is untestable for three independent reasons (PHY-01 no machine → the target may be
unreachable; PHY-05 no stochastic model → the CI is degenerate; PHY-16 no duration/pass fraction),
and PHY-07 means it is also **non-discriminating**: a stub can pass it. M1's is untestable because
"the oracle lands flat-top through the disturbance set" names a disturbance set that omits the
disturbance the project exists to reject (PHY-12), and "open-loop run demonstrably disrupts" has no
disruption model to disrupt with (PHY-06). Recommended: promote M0 and M1 gates to named
falsifiers (F-BURN-0, F-SHAPE-1) with pre-registered numbers, so DoD clause 1 is defined from
session one.

**One thing that is right and should not be touched:** §0's honesty framing, and the decision to make the tiers explicit about what is real versus decorative. The corrected growth rate (10–100 ms) does not weaken the central claim — an LLM at 1–3 s is still 20–60 e-foldings too late — so the spec can afford to be accurate here and lose nothing.

## 3 · Verdict

**Is §3 buildable cold? No.** A cold session opening `KICKOFF_M0.md` can build M0's tier-0 + PID + MC + memcmp
oracle (modulo QC-PHYSICS's M0 blockers). It **cannot** build §3, because §3 does not currently decide:
what the EKF's model is, whether MPPI is online or offline, whether the policy is MLP or GRU, whether it
runs on CPU or GPU, what it is rewarded for, what it observes beyond a prose list, what randomization it
survives, which seeds it may not touch, or what "beating the null" means operationally. Each of those is a
fork in the build, and several of them (CTL-02 determinism, CTL-05 clairvoyance, CTL-20 train-env parity)
are the kind that are cheap now and structural later — exactly the class this pre-build pass exists to catch.

The spec's *architecture* is sound: writs-not-actuators, innovation-gated tokenization, a named innovation
source, a null for every organ, receipts over claims. The defects are almost all **under-specification at
the points where the parent projects paid for the knowledge** — and the parent's ledgers already contain
the answers, including the negative ones the spec skipped.

**Top 3 risks**

1. **M2 stalls exactly where the parent stalled** (CTL-04, CTL-08). The spec inherits solver-then-distill as
   proven doctrine when the parent's own record is: teacher **proven**, DAgger **proven**, end-to-end
   distillation of the compound partially-observable case a **measured null (0/16)**, and the shipped GREEN
   artifact a θ-predictor over an analytic base. fusion's disturbance set is that same compound case, its
   plant is unstable (worse for covariate shift), and F-NULL-C is its only exit with no branch for losing.
   *Mitigation: the pre-registered M2 ladder, with rung (a) sufficient for the gate.*
2. **The control premise rests on an unmodeled and mis-stated timescale** (CTL-01, CTL-15). Without passive
   conductors, γ is 100–1000× larger and the loop is hopeless; with them (and with QC-PHYSICS's corrected
   γ⁻¹ ≈ 10–100 ms), the *stated* justification for a neural controller collapses into the null's strongest
   territory. Both the physics and the argument need fixing, and they need fixing **together** —
   fixing only the physics leaves §0 selling LQ's job as RL's necessity.
3. **The gate metrics are not yet metrics** (CTL-07, CTL-08, CTL-10, CTL-12, CTL-14, CTL-14b). "Beats the
   null on disruption rate + tracking RMS at matched compute" currently has: no disruption model, no reward,
   no dispersion envelope, no held-out seed law, no CRN, an inapplicable CI on one of its two terms, no
   split-outcome rule, a spine-shutdown escape hatch both arms can farm, and **no mechanical fence keeping
   true state out of the observation vector**. A gate that cannot be lost is not a falsifier, and this
   project's entire epistemology is falsifiers.

**Cheapest high-value fixes, in order:** (1) the M2 ladder + a `contracts/objective.toml`; (2) one D-entry
putting the policy on the CPU in fixed precision (kills CTL-02 and CTL-16 together); (3)
`contracts/dispersions.toml` + a code-enforced held-out seed set, written **before** M0's PID is tuned,
because after that it is retroactive; (4) the `statecheck` gate (CTL-14b) — one ctest, and it is what makes
every other control number mean anything; (5) writ `id`/`rev`/`ttl`/`cancel` + governor-side accept receipts
and objection window; (6) rename the harm dial and put it in risk order.
